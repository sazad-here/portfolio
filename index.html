Numeracy screener answers

The study opens with three numeracy questions (Schwartz et al., 1997); two of three correct are needed to continue.

  1. A fair six-sided die is rolled 1,000 times. How many times would it come up even (2, 4 or 6)? 500
  2. A lottery prize has a 1% chance of winning. If 1,000 people each buy one ticket, how many win? 10
  3. A sweepstakes car has a 1 in 1,000 chance of winning. What percent of tickets win? 0.1 (type 0.1, meaning 0.1%)

Approach

How every project is built

Find real data

Public health records, hospital filings, survey data and ERP transactions, never toy examples.

Clean it honestly

Duplicates, gaps and suppressed values are handled explicitly and documented, not hidden.

Model the question

Regression, optimisation, decision trees and clustering, chosen for the decision at hand.

Verify, then share

Every headline number is recomputed from the source, then published as something people can explore.
