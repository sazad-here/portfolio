⚖️ Watching Obesity Spread, 2011 → 2024

Every state, every year. Press play and the map recolours as adult obesity rises. The scatter moves in step: states with more physically inactive adults sit higher. Hover a state on either chart to highlight it on both.

Tile map: one square per state, laid out roughly geographically so each state is the same size. Colour scale is fixed across all years so changes are real, not rescaled.
