🩺 The Race Between Causes of Death, 1999 → 2016

Ten leading causes re-rank year by year as their death rates rise and fall. Choose the U.S. or any state, switch between age-adjusted rates and raw death counts, and press play.

Age-adjusted rate = deaths per 100,000, standardised to the U.S. 2000 age structure, so states with older populations don't look worse just for being older. Raw deaths show the burden in people.
