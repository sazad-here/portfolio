🌍 Hunger Over Time, 1990 → 2014

All 130 countries in the Global Hunger Index dataset, animated across five periods. Each bubble is a country, and bubble size is the third indicator. Press play to watch the world move, or click any bubble to follow its path.

The five countries from the Tableau dashboard (Burundi, Chad, Ethiopia, Bangladesh, India) are pinned to start. Periods: undernourishment 1990-92 … 2011-13, child underweight 1988-92 … 2009-13, under-5 mortality 1990 … 2012.

What to look for

  • Burundi moves the wrong way. Undernourishment climbs from 44% to 67% while most of the world drifts down and left.
  • Bangladesh and India cut undernourishment sharply, but child underweight stays high (37% and 31%). This is the gap the dashboard pointed out.
  • Switch the Y axis to under-5 mortality and you'll see Chad sitting well above countries with similar hunger levels.
Source: 2014GHIdata.xlsx (sheet 2014GHIData), IFPRI Global Hunger Index. Values marked as estimates in the source are included. Countries missing a value for a period fade out until data resumes.
