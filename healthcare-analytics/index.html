💰 Hospital Profit Decision Support System

ExcelScenario modelling

An Excel model of hospital revenue, expenses and net income for five states. Ohio fell far short of an $850K net-income target, so three scenarios were built to close the gap. Every number is a live formula.

Net income by state

Base model, per 1,000 population

Getting Ohio over the line

Net income by scenario vs the $850K target
  • Repricing (Plan B) gets furthest: $959K, from raising inpatient, ER and outpatient unit rates.
  • Volume growth (Plan A) also clears the target at $897K without touching prices, because fees and depreciation are fixed and supplies is the only cost that scales with activity.

🌍 Global Hunger Index Dashboard

Tableau2014 GHI

Four indicators behind the 2014 Global Hunger Index, compared across five countries. The same countries appear in the same order in every panel, so you can read across.

  • One component can drive the score: Burundi leads on undernourishment (67.3%) yet has the lowest child-underweight rate.
  • The index doesn't track child deaths: Chad and Ethiopia score almost the same, but Chad's under-5 mortality is more than double.
  • Low hunger, high child undernutrition: Bangladesh and India have the lowest scores and the highest underweight rates.

❤️ Framingham Heart Risk Analysis

SAS Visual Statisticsk-meansDecision tree5,209 patients

k-means clustering (k = 3) finds three cardiovascular risk profiles. A decision tree then traces how risk factors compound toward death from coronary heart disease (CHD).

Risk factors compound

Share of deaths from CHD along the riskiest branch of the tree

What the clusters say

  • Smoking costs the most life: heavy smokers (25 cig/day) die about 10.4 years earlier than the low-risk group.
  • High BP and cholesterol (183 mmHg, 259 mg/dL) cost about 1.6 years.
  • Blood pressure is the top predictor (importance 94.7). The tree splits on it first, and high BP alone more than doubles the CHD share.
  • The last leaf holds only 25 patients, so read its 56% as indicative, not precise.
