Reading the tree

  • Age is the first split: under-45s buy at 52.2%, everyone else at 15.8%.
  • Affluence comes next on both sides. The best segment, under 45 with affluence ≥ 15, buys at 91.7% (2,390 of 2,605).
  • The largest segment is the least likely: 64,885 customers aged 45+ with low or missing affluence buy at 11.0%.
  • Gender matters inside segments: wherever the tree separates women from others, women buy more often (53.6% vs 23.9%, 75.8% vs 49.6%, 43.4% vs 17.7%).
