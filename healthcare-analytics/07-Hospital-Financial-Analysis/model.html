🏥 444 Reports, 432 Hospitals: The Financial Explorer

Every California hospital financial report in the 2020–21 HCAI extract, one dot each. Take the guided tour through the executive questions, or pick your own axes. Then merge the duplicate filings and watch the counts correct themselves.

Debt ratio = total liabilities ÷ total assets · operating margin = net income ÷ net patient revenue · payer mix = Medicare + Medi-Cal share of net patient revenue. Margins beyond ±60% are pinned to the edge (hollow dots).
