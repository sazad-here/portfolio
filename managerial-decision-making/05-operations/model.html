🚚 Operations Optimizer

Three everyday operations problems, each solved live in your browser: who to schedule, what to ship where, and how to invest cash over five years. Change any input and watch the optimal plan rebuild itself.

Formulations match the original Excel Solver workbooks; at their default inputs the in-browser solver returns Solver's answers exactly: 23 workers, $176,050 and $286,792.

👷 Staff scheduling

Each worker works five days in a row, then has two off. Set how many are needed each day; the solver finds the smallest workforce (whole people) that covers every day.
