💼 What Makes a Couple Save for Retirement?

194 couples, one question: what predicts the share of income they put into tax-deferred retirement plans? Toggle predictors in and out and the regression refits instantly. Drop the one that isn't pulling its weight, then plan for a couple of your own.

Matches the team's Excel analysis: with all four predictors R² = 0.453 and Children is not significant (p = 0.954); dropping it leaves R² unchanged with F = 52.47.
